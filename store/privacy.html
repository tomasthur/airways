AIRWAYS Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Developer: Tomas Thur · Contact: thur.tomas@gmail.com

This privacy policy applies to the Android game AIRWAYS (package com.tomasthur.airways) on Google Play. It describes what data the game accesses, collects, uses, and shares.

What AIRWAYS is

AIRWAYS is an airline-career game. You name a carrier, fly visual hops, earn in-game credits, and optionally buy aircraft or an ads-removal pass through Google Play Billing. Full-screen ads may show before a flight unless you own that pass. An optional rewarded video can grant extra in-game cash.

Data we collect

  • Airline name and email — entered at registration. The airline name is shown in the hub, on landing reports, and on the public leaderboard. Email is used to contact you about the game and to identify your airline record. It is not shown on the leaderboard.
  • Career stats — successful flights, crashes, losses, and in-game cash. These feed the leaderboard score.
  • A random player id — created on your device so your airline row can be updated.
  • Device save data — progress, language, copilot, and purchase flags stored locally on the device (not in a developer cloud save besides the leaderboard row).

We do not collect payment card numbers. Google Play processes real-money purchases.

Data shared with others

  • Convex (our leaderboard backend) stores airline name, email, player id, and career stats so the world board can update.
  • Google AdMob may use the advertising ID, IP address, device and app info, and approximate location to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where consent allows. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing handles product identifiers and purchase receipts. See Google Play’s terms and privacy policy.

The public leaderboard shows airline name and stats, not email.

Ads and children

AIRWAYS contains ads and in-app purchases. It is not directed at children. Do not create an airline for a child under 13 / under 16 where required by local law.

In the EEA/UK we show a consent form (UMP) before personalized ads. If you decline, ads may still show in a non-personalized form. Buying Remove ads skips the full-screen interstitial before flights; the optional rewarded “More Money” video remains available.

How long we keep data

Leaderboard rows stay until you erase the airline or ask us to delete them. Local save data stays on the device until you clear app storage or uninstall. Purchase records are kept by Google according to Google Play policy; restoring Play purchases can re-grant owned aircraft and Remove ads on a new install.

Your rights and deletion

In the game, Restart career deletes your airline from the leaderboard and returns you to registration. Google Play purchases can be restored afterwards.

You may also email thur.tomas@gmail.com to access, correct, or delete your airline row. We will delete associated leaderboard data. Please say the airline name and the email you registered with.

If you are in the EEA/UK you also have GDPR rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability) and the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in Slovakia, Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR).

Security

Leaderboard traffic uses HTTPS. We do not store passwords. Do not put sensitive data in the airline name field.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, we will update this page and the date above.
