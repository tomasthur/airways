EN:
AIRWAYS is a visual-flight airline career. Name your carrier, start at Bratislava, and fly real hops across a living map.

You sit in a 3D cockpit with a full preflight checklist, throttle, yoke, gear, flaps, lights and brakes. Copilot can take the busy work. Coach can talk you through the first flights. Then you fly it yourself — climb, cruise, PAPI, and a landing that actually scores.

Earn charter credits on every hop. Unlock cities. Buy the next aircraft in the hangar, from a Cessna 172RG up through turboprops and jets to the Airbus A380. Pay with in-game cash or a one-time Google Play unlock. A €2.99 Remove ads pass skips the full-screen ad before each flight; the optional More Money rewarded video stays if you still want extra cash.

Features
• Career hub with a pan-and-zoom world map
• Named airline on a live leaderboard
• Fleet: Cessna 172RG, Piper Seneca, King Air 350, ATR 72, Dash 8 Q400, Embraer E190, A320neo, 787-9, 777-300ER, A380
• Day and night visual flying, weather and ATC
• Preflight checklist, copilot and on-screen coach
• English, Slovak, German and Chinese

Start small. Keep the rubber on the centerline. Build an airline.
